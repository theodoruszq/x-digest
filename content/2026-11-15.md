> quick fixes considered harmful: you might be making your agents write worse code when you ask for the smallest or quickest possible change
> what seems like a harmless [[bandaid|n. 创可贴（临时补丁）]] may lead to that pattern being copied by the next agent. before you know it that pattern has spread everywhere

**Steve Yegge's “hot takes” from his talks: slow your AI down to human speed, and you probably don't need a software factory** · [Original](https://x.com/Steve_Yegge/status/2121671447810888185) · ♥ 1.1k · 2026-11-14T18:27:46Z
> First hot take: I tell everyone to slow down. … I strongly believe in [[pacing|v. 控制节奏]] AI outputs to human speeds. … almost nobody is _actually_ going fast with AI. Engineering still moves at engineering speeds, and business moves even slower.
> My agents have, at times, overwhelmed my players with features, my accountant with emails, my [[merge queue|合并队列（等待合入主分支的代码排队）]] with tickets, and my machines with load. … they've launched broken OSS without permission.
> Hot take #2: Should you buy a software factory? … No. … Factories take an extraordinary amount of [[upkeep|n. 维护]]. They turn into garbage mountains unless you put a frightening amount of time and tokens into [[curating|v. 精心整理]] them. I had to burn my previous five factories down …
💡 “软件工厂”指一整套让很多 agent 自动写、测、发代码的流水线。Yegge 自己在用它运营一款网络游戏，坦白讲翻车经历：agent 给玩家塞太多功能、给会计狂发邮件、未经允许发布有问题的开源项目。结论是“自己搭，别买”，而且要放慢。
