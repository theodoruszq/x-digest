**4. An Anthropic engineer's prompt for asking AI to go "deeper, not bigger"** · [Original](https://x.com/nichochar/status/2110589417811579362) · ♥ 107 · 2026-10-15T04:31:44Z
> I love seeing how others prompt. This one stood out to me:
> "I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one. Before rewriting it: (1) say what a top professional in this field would do here that I'm not asking for; (2) name the rawest material they would start from instead of what has already been [[digested|adj. （被别人）消化加工过的]] for me; (3) name the specific methods and tools they would use; (4) say what, other than you or me, would show the result is right. Then rewrite my prompt to ask for exactly that."
💡 出自 Claude 团队工程负责人 Felix Rieseberg 10/13 发的一篇教程，讲用 AI 时怎样"更有野心"（原帖 ♥ 1.9 万、收藏 4 万）。这段提示词可以直接复制：先让 AI 说出"行家会怎么做"、该从哪些一手材料入手、怎么验证结果，再让它重写你的提示词。Thorsten Ball 也说最惊艳的是这些看似简单的提示词背后的第一性原理思考（♥ 1k）。
