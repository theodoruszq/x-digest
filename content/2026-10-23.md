**2. Two tennis players turned phone videos into Claude coaching, and it found their weak strokes on its own** · [Original](https://x.com/BrainsAndTennis/status/2113349053425832420) · ♥ 826 · 2026-10-22T19:17:33Z
> We chained open-source models post-trained on tennis. This cut each broadcast down to live play, found and tracked the players, ball, court, and labeled every stroke with its type and a 65-joint skeleton. We then post-processed this data into 30+ [[biomechanical|adj. 生物力学的]] traits across serves, forehands and backhands, [[normalized|v. 归一化（按身高体型、机位换算到同一标准）]] for each player's build and the camera.
> … Then we gave Claude all the measurements with a blank context.
> - Without being told, it found my weaker side (forehand) and Richard's (backhand)
> - It joined differences that looked separate into one [[root cause|n. 根本原因]] per stroke
> - My own diagnosis of my forehand was wrong. Claude's makes a lot more sense
💡 收藏 975，比点赞还多。作者 Peter Wang 和 @nicochristie 先用 34 小时职业比赛录像（德约、阿尔卡拉斯、辛纳等 11 人）建立基准，再拿自己用手机拍的视频对比。思路值得借鉴：先用专门模型把视频变成数字，再交给 Claude 解读。效果是作者自己的体验，没有严格评测。
