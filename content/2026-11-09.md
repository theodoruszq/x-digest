**4. Eric S. Raymond: he deliberately keeps LLM use primitive — no subagents, no skills, no “software factory” — and sees no productivity loss** · [Original](https://x.com/esrtweet/status/2119518719923585140) · ♥ 1.2k · 2026-11-08T19:53:36Z
> I have deliberately chosen to keep the way I interact with large language models extremely simple, even [[primitive|adj. 原始的]]. I don't get all tangled up in spawning subagents, accumulating skills, or trying to build a software factory that runs while I'm not there. …
> I have yet to see any convincing evidence that this is losing me productivity.
> … it seems to me that all these [[elaborations|n. 繁复的附加设计]] are ways to cope with the frontier models not yet being quite smart enough. But the models are always getting smarter.
> … why clutter my brain with procedures that are likely to have only a short [[half-life|n. 半衰期（这里指有效期）]] of usefulness? …
> Yes, I'm applying [[the bitter lesson|“苦涩的教训”：AI 领域名言，通用方法+更多算力终将胜过人工设计的技巧]] to my own workflow.
💡 和昨天 Brian Armstrong 的多 agent 队列形成对照：ESR（《大教堂与集市》作者）认为这些流程只是在弥补模型不够聪明，模型变强后就会过时。

**6. Nous Research ships a Security plugin for Hermes Agent: maps the attack surface, splits review across subagents, confirms bugs with reproductions** · [Original](https://x.com/NousResearch/status/2119501958981857543) · ♥ 975 · 2026-11-08T18:47:00Z
> Hermes Agent can now review your codebase for security bugs with the new official Security plugin by @mark_s.
> Hermes first maps what an attacker could reach and which inputs it trusts. It splits the code across parallel subagents, checks every candidate finding against the code, and traces whether an attacker can actually [[exploit|v. 利用（漏洞）]] it.
> Turn on test confirmation and Hermes writes a small [[reproduction|n. 复现用例]] for each finding and runs it in a temporary copy of the repo. Each report records the evidence and the findings Hermes [[ruled out|排除了]], and exports to [[SARIF|一种静态分析结果的标准格式]] for your existing tools.
💡 开源 agent 的实用插件：重点是“先复现再报告”，减少 AI 安全审查常见的误报。
