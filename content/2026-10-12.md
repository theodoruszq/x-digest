**1. Karpathy: the gap in understanding what AI can do is widening into a "sharp funnel"** · [Original](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2109361546505966046) · ♥ 23k · 2026-10-11T19:12:37Z
> …this gap in shared understanding of LLM capability is *widening*. It's now less "two groups of people speaking past each other" and more a sharp [[funnel|n. 漏斗]].
> - [[Napkin math|粗略估算]] somewhere around 6B people (~75% of the population) have barely come in contact with LLMs at all.
> - Around 1-2B (~20%) are casual and infrequent users of free-tier ChatGPT-like products. This group sees [[derpy|adj. 傻乎乎的]] chatbots and treats them a bit like a better Google search, a writing aid…
> - …Somewhere around 20M people (0.2%) see first-hand that large, complex projects that used to take them weeks/months can now be completed by agents with a prompt. Building apps, copying apps, translating apps, decompiling apps from binaries…
> - And finally we get to the ~5,000 people with access to frontier-grade systems internally… swarms of thousands of agents collaborating over weeks on software mega projects: minting [[zero days|n. 零日漏洞（尚未公开、未修补的漏洞）]], running cyber attacks and defenses at machine speeds, discovering new science, advancing the frontier of mathematics…
> …The general public has mostly not interacted with these systems. When they have, it looks like a derpy chatbot. The majority of professionals still see only a modest [[uplift|n. 提升]]. And a small [[sliver|n. 一小部分]] of professionals are experiencing the [[vertigo|n. 眩晕感]] of the curve going vertical. And it is all happening at the same time.
💡 他认为差距来自三点：① 问题越大、周期越长，AI 的作用越明显；② 能力"参差不齐"，在数学、代码这类数字化、能自动验证对错的领域最强；③ 使用权限不同（免费版、付费版、实验室内部）。收藏 1.57 万，几乎和点赞一样多。Ethan Mollick 补充说，就连这 5,000 人也主要把先进模型用在网络安全、数学和 AI 研发上，其他领域还有大量问题没人去解（♥ 362）。

**5. AnyPS5: an open-source project that ports PS5 games to run natively on PC** · [Original](https://x.com/RoundtableSpace/status/2109437643226226877) · ♥ 42 · 2026-10-12T00:15:00Z
> AnyPS5 [[ports|v. 移植]] PS5 [[executables|n. 可执行程序]] to run natively on Windows and Linux. Not an emulator. It's at ~28K stars and 2.4K forks.
> * relinker → turns PS5 executables into native PC format
> * libraries → PS5 system libraries rebuilt from scratch
> * graphics → [[shaders|n. 着色器（在显卡上运行的图形程序）]] recompiled to SPIR-V
> * reality check → so far a small 2D game is shown at 60 fps
> → no emulation, no separate runtime → ships no firmware, keys or Sony code → pitched for preservation and [[interoperability|n. 互通性]]
💡 项目地址 github.com/boykopovar/AnyPS5。模拟器是在 PC 上"假装"成一台 PS5；它则把游戏程序本身改写成 PC 程序。OC3D 等媒体报道它已实现 100% 的 PS5 GPU 着色器指令转换。目前只跑起来一个小 2D 游戏，离玩大作还很远。点赞不多，但 GitHub 星数说明热度很高，和 10/11 的 REA、ArtCraft 同属最近的逆向、重写开源热潮。
