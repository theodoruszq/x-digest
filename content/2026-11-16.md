**Guillermo Rauch open-sources xeet: renders an X post to a pixel-perfect PNG in ~1ms, no browser needed — and the repo is maintained by agents** · [Original](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2122184794209714368) · ♥ 545 · 2026-11-16T04:27:37Z
> A [[hermetic|adj. 完全封闭的（不依赖任何外部环境）]], pixel-perfect X post renderer in safe Rust / wasm. Zero browser, network, filesystem, system fonts. Renders a post to PNG in ~1ms …
> Rendering with xeet is 100x-1000x faster than booting up a browser and taking a screenshot, yet it's just as accurate.
> … xeet is structured as an "autorepo." An [[oracle|n. 判定程序（给出“标准答案”的参照）]] boots up Chrome, and verifies tens of thousands of text layout rules. A CI [[canary|n. 金丝雀监测（盯着变化、提前报警的自动检查）]] then watches the evolution of the original, and files signed PRs for agents to act on.
💡 Vercel CEO 的新开源项目。亮点不在“截图”本身，而在做法：用真 Chrome 当标准答案自动比对上万条排版规则，X 一改样式就自动开 PR 让 agent 去修——正好呼应上午 Yuchen Jin 说的“先建验证器，再让 agent 写代码”。
