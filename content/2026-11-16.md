**Nicolas Bustamante: most SaaS should become an MCP server — let ChatGPT or Claude do the chatting, and let customers pay for the tokens** · [Original](https://x.com/nicbstme/status/2121690979904446885) · ♥ 1.9k · 2026-11-14T19:45:23Z
> I came to the conclusion that most SaaS should become an MCP server.
> we're getting the economics of AI software backwards. A lot of AI startups are building their own chat interfaces, agents, and workflows, essentially reselling tokens from the big labs at low to negative [[gross margins|n. 毛利率]].
> … Let ChatGPT, Claude, or Copilot handle the conversation, reasoning, and [[orchestration|n. 编排（调度多个步骤/工具）]]. Let the customer pay for the tokens.
> The SaaS company keeps the valuable stuff: [[proprietary|adj. 专有的、独家的]] data, integrations, business logic, permissions, compliance, and the [[system of record|权威数据系统（存放“正式”数据的那套系统）]].
> … The next generation of SaaS might not have much of a UI at all. Just an incredibly good MCP server.
💡 MCP 是让 AI 助手调用外部工具和数据的通用接口。观点是：软件公司自己做聊天机器人等于转卖大模型的 token、赚不到钱；不如只提供数据和能力，让用户已经在用的 AI 来调用。作者举了 HR 软件的例子（薪资对标、调薪预算、审批流程）。
