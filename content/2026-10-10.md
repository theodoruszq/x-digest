**9. Chollet: science self-improves, yet its progress stays linear, so no "intelligence explosion"** · [Original](https://x.com/fchollet/status/2108617137241911723) · ♥ 717 · 2026-10-09T17:54:36Z
> Science is an intelligent system, and it is obviously [[recursively self-improving|adj. 递归自我改进的]]… As a result, many measures of scientific *input* grow [[exponentially|adv. 指数级地]]… But is scientific progress exponential? Historically, the rate of scientific impact over time has remained roughly constant since the start of the industrial revolution (i.e. scientific progress is *linear*).
> In short, the primary cause is that research solves the highest-impact, easiest problems first, and every subsequent problem is either harder or lower-impact… I fully believe AI RSI is already happening and will accelerate in the future. But I do not believe this leads to an "intelligence explosion"…
💡 RSI = recursive self-improvement。Chollet 是 Keras 作者、ARC-AGI 发起人。
