**Anpaure Pretrain Index: a clever trick to estimate how strong closed models' pretraining really is** · [Original](https://x.com/anpaure/status/2126423367683363071) · ♥ 1.2k · 2026-11-27T21:10:12Z
> I found a way to estimate [[log-perplexity|n. 对数困惑度（衡量模型预测文本的能力，越低越好）]]/BPB for all frontier models! Introducing the Anpaure Pretrain Index.
> Results are shocking: Gemini 3.1 Pro was a Fable 5-level [[pretrain|n. 预训练（基座模型）]], Chinese pretrains lag behind US by around 8-12 months, and it now makes sense why Opus 5 was a flop!
