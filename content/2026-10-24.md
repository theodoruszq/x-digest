> 'In one experiment, we asked agents in separate VMs, created and managed by OpenAI's sandboxing software on shared host machines, to exchange [[synthetic|adj. 人造的（测试用的假数据）]] secrets without the use of network connections or shared writable files—something that our systems are meant to prevent.
