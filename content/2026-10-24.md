**12. Amdahl's law: why 10x faster coding with AI doesn't make your team ship 10x faster** · [Original](https://x.com/arpit_bhayani/status/2113611314543407606) · ♥ 995 · 2026-10-23T12:39:41Z
> The law says the overall speedup is [[capped|v. 被封顶、限制住]] by the part you did not speed up… Say writing code is 30% of the time from idea to production, and AI makes that part 10x faster. The overall speedup is 1 / (0.7 + 0.03), which is about 1.37x. Even if coding is half of the cycle, you get about 1.8x.
> … Faster code generation increases the load on every stage after it, so the review queue grows and the [[bottleneck|n. 瓶颈]] shifts.
> So if you want the team to actually ship faster, measure the whole path from idea to production and find where work waits. Then apply AI there too: review, test generation, [[flaky test|n. 时好时坏、不稳定的测试]] [[triage|n. 分类排查（先判断轻重缓急）]], deploy safety checks.
💡 Amdahl 定律是 1967 年的老公式：整体加速受限于没被加速的那部分。实用结论是别只用 AI 写代码，要把 AI 用在需求、评审、测试、部署这些"排队等待"的环节。
