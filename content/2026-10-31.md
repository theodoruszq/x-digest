**4. A simple agent habit: when it goes in circles, keep only the best result and restart in a fresh session** · [Original](https://x.com/undefinedKi/status/2116185563501916409) · ♥ 92 · 2026-10-30T15:08:49Z
> Your agent gets dumber the longer it works on a task. …
> The agent gets [[anchored|adj. 被（先前结果）锚定、思路被框住]] to its own past attempts. The more history it sees, the smaller its edits get.
> The fix is to make it forget. Every so often, close the session, keep only the best result so far, and start a fresh session from it.
> On the same budget, that beat a single agent by up to 39% and parallel agents by up to 15%.
> To do it yourself: when your agent starts going in circles, ask it to save the best version and what it learned, then open a fresh session and give it only that.
💡 据 X 新闻汇总，出自伯克利和斯坦福研究者的一篇 arXiv 论文。点赞不多但马上能用：让 agent 把"目前最好版本 + 学到的东西"写进一个文件，再开新会话只喂这个文件。开发者 @ccorcos 说（♥185）："This is such a good idea, I'm embarrassed I didn't think of it."
