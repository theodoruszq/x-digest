**"Cognitive surrender": in a study of 1,372 people, wrong AI answers cut accuracy — yet made them more confident** · [Original](https://x.com/thesupermannx/status/2124450520056377395) · ♥ 2.4k · 2026-11-22T10:30:49Z
> Researchers ran three experiments across 1,372 people and 9,593 reasoning trials. They gave participants access to an AI assistant, but secretly [[rigged|v. 暗中操纵]] the AI to be accurate on some trials and [[subtly|adv. 不易察觉地]] wrong on others.
> People [[consulted|v. 求助于]] the AI on more than 50% of the trials. When the AI was right, human accuracy jumped by 25 percentage points. When the AI was wrong, accuracy crashed by 15 percentage points. …
> Using the AI made people measurably more confident in their answers—even when the AI fed them completely wrong information.
> The researchers tried to break the habit. They added time pressure. They gave participants live feedback and financial incentives … It didn't work.
💡 据 X 新闻汇总，这是 Steven Shaw 和 Gideon Nave 的 2026 年预印本（还没经过同行评审）。发帖人结尾那句“AI 正在悄悄取代思考本身”是他自己的引申。实用启示：先写下自己的答案再问 AI，并对 AI 的回答主动找反例。
