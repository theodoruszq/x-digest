**1. Lauren Tan: an "[[inverse pyramid|倒金字塔]]" for trusting your agents' code** · [Original](https://x.com/poteto/status/2111133080526495985) · ♥ 4.2k · 2026-10-16T16:32:04Z
> how do you trust your agents' work more? copy this post/link and give it to your @bot or agent!
> 1. codebase improvements: … better architecture, stronger types and [[invariants|n. 不变式（代码里永远必须成立的条件）]], clear boundaries. agents love to copy what exists and take the shortest path, so make the easy thing the right thing. [[categorically|adv. 彻底地、从根本上]] eliminate problems through code and you won't need to think about it again.
> 2. static analysis: lint rules, tests, CI, verification (eg TLA+). very token efficient ways to catch issues ahead of time.
> 3. prose: comments, rules, AGENTS.md, skills - anything that can't be enforced and requires agent judgment. use it minimally …
> 4. human review: the hardest to scale. … spend that effort on the highest [[leverage|n. 杠杆（投入少、收益大）]] point, designing an environment where agents do the right thing by default.
> this forms an inverse pyramid: you want most of your effort at the top, least at the bottom. codebase improvements are [[deterministic|adj. 确定性的（每次结果都一样）]] …
💡 收藏 7.7k，几乎是点赞的两倍。昨天她分享了 pstack，今天讲的是背后的思路：与其靠写规则、靠人审代码，不如先把代码库本身改得"智能体照着抄也不会错"。帖子后半段还给了一段可以直接交给智能体的提示词：让它翻你过去的代码评审意见和对话记录，找出智能体最常犯的错，再提出改造方案。Morgan Linton 说把这条丢给自己的 Grok Bot，它马上学会了，还举出了一次本可以避免的错误（♥ 706）。
