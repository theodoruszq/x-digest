> A new Anthropic-written safety bill is circulating on [[Capitol Hill|n. 美国国会]]. … As written, however, it will eventually sweep in most of Silicon Valley, damaging the startup ecosystem.
> The bill defines the "frontier" as any model trained on 10²⁵ [[FLOPs|n. 浮点运算次数（衡量训练用了多少算力）]] — the EU standard from 2023. Biden's executive order, California's SB 53, and New York's RAISE Act used 10²⁶ (ten times more). 10²⁷ is where the frontier actually is today (e.g., GPT-6 Astra).
> The definition is also [[cumulative|adj. 累计的]]: it adds FLOPs from fine-tuning, reinforcement learning, or other material modifications. …
> A [[stale|adj. 过时的]] 2023 cutoff favors [[incumbents|n. 在位者（已经站稳的大公司）]] who already clear the bar and can absorb the costs. It [[hobbles|v. 束缚、拖累]] the open model ecosystem that threatens Anthropic's pricing power.
