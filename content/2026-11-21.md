**MIT paper: harnesses aren't a crutch for weak models — they let LLMs generalize far beyond training** · [Original](https://x.com/a1zhang/status/2123774098241147225) · ♥ 1.1k · 2026-11-20T13:42:57Z
> Harnesses used to be a [[crutch|n. 拐杖（权宜依靠）]] for weak LLMs. In 2026 and onward, with stronger models, harnesses now offer a mechanism for composing an LLM's existing and new capabilities to generalize well beyond their training environments.
> … A common idea floating around is that LLMs will absorb harnesses and make them [[obsolete|adj. 过时的]]. We disagree, and argue that we haven't sufficiently explored what harnesses can provide.
> The full paper, logs, codebase, and data are available
💡 论文名 *LM Harnesses are Compositional Generalizers*（作者 Alex Zhang、Souradip Chakraborty、Omar Khattab，就是 DSPy 的作者）。“harness”指包在模型外面的那层框架：工具调用、子 agent、循环等。Sebastian Raschka 补充：模型本来就是在特定 harness 里微调的，所以 harness 不会被模型“吸收”（[链接](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2123853084216455549)）。
