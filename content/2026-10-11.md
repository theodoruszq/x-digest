**1. Satya Nadella: treat AI models like insider risks, and keep the controls outside the model** · [Original](https://x.com/satyanadella/status/2108931348857827686) · ♥ 6.4k · 2026-10-10T14:43:10Z
> …we are deploying these complex agentic systems and models, with access to our most sensitive data and giving them the ability to take [[mission-critical|adj. 关键任务的，出错代价极高的]] actions on our behalf! … we need to separate the supply of intelligence from the authority over it.
> Treating frontier closed and open weight models like [[insider risks|n. 内部人员风险（"内鬼"风险）]] is a way to build such a system. Not because they are necessarily [[malicious|adj. 恶意的]], but because any sufficiently capable actor with access to important systems can make mistakes or be [[compromised|adj. 被攻破的，被入侵的]]…
> …the controls that govern what a model can access and what actions it can take must sit outside the model.
> Containment: We must assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start. Think of it like an emergency brake. An authorized person should always be able to pause or shut down a model mid-task.
> The most trustworthy Super Intelligence system will not be the one with the model we trust most. It will be the one that enables us to trust the model the least.
💡 Nadella 是微软 CEO，这是他在 X 上发的长文《Models as Insider Risks in the Super Intelligence Era》，收藏 6.9k，比点赞还多。核心意思：别指望模型自己可靠，要像防"内鬼"一样给 AI 限权限、留日志、随时能叫停。Musk 转发称"Interesting piece"（♥ 14.6k），Chamath 也称这是"非常重要的观察"。

**2. Chamath: AI-built open-source clones of Adobe's apps make software IP "worthless"** · [Original](https://x.com/theallinpod/status/2108897316086095923) · ♥ 2.4k · 2026-10-10T12:27:56Z
> "I think that IP and software was effectively rendered worthless two days ago. There have been people that [[decompiled|v. 反编译]] the entire Big 5… Adobe has five very, very important products in their product suite, and these folks basically decompiled it, distilled it, and published open source versions of these things."
> "…when all of these things are [[headless|adj. 无界面的（只通过 API/代理调用）]] and you can wrap them in agent tools and you can connect them via MCP to other experiences, the value of IP no longer exists in software. It doesn't exist."
💡 他说的是 ArtCraft 项目：开发者 Brandon Thomas 借助 Claude Opus 5.5，用 Rust 写出 7 款对标 Adobe 的免费开源应用（PhotoCraft 对标 Photoshop、VectorCraft 对标 Illustrator、FilmCraft 对标 Premiere 等）。项目方自称是 clean-room 重写（不碰原代码、只按公开规格实现），和 Chamath 说的"反编译"并不一样；目前仍是早期 alpha，bug 不少。出自 All-In 播客，收藏 1.3k。

**3. levelsio's nostalgia side project: Windows 3.1 and XP in your browser, 80,000 players in a day** · [Original](https://x.com/levelsio/status/2108957488410161442) · ♥ 328 · 2026-10-10T16:27:02Z
> Two years ago I started working on these crazy [[nostalgia|n. 怀旧]] projects, kind of randomly, because I was bored and wanted to do something different than just build another SaaS or AI app/website. I wanted to try to run Windows 3.1 inside a web browser…
> And then I spent 6 months trying to [[reverse engineer|v. 逆向工程（拆解分析现成软件）]] Windows XP to run on v86… very difficult (because it's so big) and only possible because modern AI like Opus 5.5 is good enough to figure that out
> …yesterday 80,000 people played around in Windows 3.1 or Windows XP inside their browsers chatting to MSN and AOL Messenger AI bots and playing 3D Pinball or playing Quake etc.
> And that was my idea for @pieter: a non-commercial [[preservation|n. （文物等的）保存]] project, kinda like an online computer museum but interactive!
💡 v86 是在浏览器里模拟 x86 电脑的开源模拟器；他把老游戏移植到 WASM（让程序直接在网页里跑的技术），再加上联机服务器。他说这是他第一个"不赚钱、也不打算赚钱"却火起来的项目。

**8. Investors now see French companies as safer than the French government** · [Original](https://x.com/KobeissiLetter/status/2108910167714582857) · ♥ 1.4k · 2026-10-10T13:19:00Z
> $241 billion worth of French [[corporate bonds|n. 公司债]] are now trading at lower [[yields|n. 收益率]] than French government bonds. This figure is up +1,621% from just $14 billion at the start of 2026.
> …internationally exposed companies such as L'Oreal and TotalEnergies are increasingly viewed as safer alternatives to French government debt, unlike French banks, which remain more exposed to [[sovereign risk|n. 主权风险（政府违约风险）]].
