Addy Osmani: if you spend all day with a coding agent, you are managing — give it a way to check its work, and never delegate the judgment

@addyosmani·♥Likes: 1.4k·Original·Nov 8, 07:32 UTC

The skills for managing agents are pretty much the skills for managing a team, and you don't need to be a manager to learn them.

If you're spending all day with a coding agent, you are managing. You're repeatedly selecting tasks, writing instructions, delegatingv. 委派、把任务交给别人, following up, evaluating results, and deciding what ships. The engineers who get the most out of agents aren't necessarily the ones who are best at prompt engineering. They're the ones who learned how to manage, intentionally or otherwise. …

Give it a way to check its work. … Provide the agent with a mechanism to validate its work, like a test suite, build, or screenshot, and have it show you the results as proof. …

Delegatev. 委派 the task, never the judgment. The agent does the work, you decide the important things: product calls, security boundaries, what ships. Treat every diff like you're going to get pagedv. 被呼叫：值班时被叫醒处理故障 for it.

💡 Addy 现在在 Anthropic 做 Claude Code。文章把管 agent 比作管团队：先给验证手段（测试、构建、截图），上下文只写仓库里看不到的东西，同一错误改两次就写进 CLAUDE.md；产品取舍、安全和是否上线仍由人决定。他也写过“cognitive surrender”（把思考外包给 AI 后，自己渐渐不会想）。这是实践建议，不是评测。

DHH: Omarchy has been extending Asahi’s Apple Silicon work — Touch ID, M3 GPU, deep sleep — but Asahi now rejects upstreaming because of “agent acceleration”

@dhh·♥Likes: 1.4k·Original·Nov 8, 09:34 UTC

I'm grateful for the work Asahi did on Apple Silicon, and in the best open source spirit, we've been expanding that with TouchID, M3 GPU, DisplayPort over USB-C, deep sleep, MacBook Neo. But thanks to agent acceleration用 AI agent 加快开发, Asahi rejects upstreamingv. 回馈上游：把改动合并回原项目 🤷

💡 Asahi 是给 Apple 芯片 Mac 移植 Linux 的志愿项目。他们在 11 月 7 日的 Linux 7.3 进度报告里说，有人从个人仓库拿走未完成代码、不署名，或用 LLM 改写来掩盖来源，因此把仍用 MIT 的部分改成 GPLv2-or-later（Wine 在 2002 年也因类似原因从 MIT 换成 LGPL）。他们还写明 broadly forbid 生成式 AI。Peter Steinberger（♥2.3k）认为这和 Asahi“打开封闭硬件”的初衷相反。DHH 随后补充：Linux 内核本来就是 GPLv2，Omarchy 本身仍是 MIT，他们对内核的改动会公开。许可和 AI 政策之争，双方都有各自立场。
