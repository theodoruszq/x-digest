@AnthropicAI·♥Likes: 3.9k·Original·Nov 24, 23:05 UTC

… over the past couple years, researchers largely moved on from that idea, for three reasons: a single neuron often fires on several unrelated inputs, the way a concept is represented can change between two layers …, and facts seem to be spread across many neurons.

… With no extra training, neurons in open-weight models helped us to detect harmful text, steerv. 引导、操控 behavior, and find hidden behaviors in finetuned models.
