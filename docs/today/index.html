John Carmack on why NeXT and 3dfx “did the right thing” with linear color — and still looked worse

@ID_AA_Carmack·♥Likes: 1.9k·Original·Nov 16, 23:53 UTC

Both NeXT and 3DFX tried to “do the right thing” with color spaces, and it wound up generally making them look worse than competing options. …

If you have 10+ bits per channel like the professional rendering systems, linear displays are The Right Thing.

Unfortunately, NeXTStation Colors were 4 bits per channel and 3DFX was 5 or 6 bits per channel, and there… it was not the right thing.

Human eyes aren’t linear intensityn. 光强、亮度 sensors. We mostly register relative changes in intensity, so we can tell the difference between 0.01 and 0.02 as a huge doubling, while 0.99 to 1.00 is almost imperceptibleadj. 察觉不到的.

💡 一篇很好懂的 gamma 科普：人眼对暗部变化更敏感，颜色位数少的时候，就该把有限的亮度档位多分给暗部，而不是平均分。
