Hempton says the only way you can get to these sorts of numbers is by selling AI as a productivity tool to businesses, meaning replacing workers. … the savings have to be about double what businesses pay for the AI: roughly $4 trillion in savings on wages a year. … roughly 10% of the OECD’s total wage billn. 工资总额 …
