Chollet: science self-improves, yet its progress stays linear, so no "intelligence explosion"

Science is an intelligent system, and it is obviously recursively self-improvingadj. 递归自我改进的… As a result, many measures of scientific *input* grow exponentiallyadv. 指数级地… But is scientific progress exponential? Historically, the rate of scientific impact over time has remained roughly constant since the start of the industrial revolution (i.e. scientific progress is *linear*).

In short, the primary cause is that research solves the highest-impact, easiest problems first, and every subsequent problem is either harder or lower-impact… I fully believe AI RSI is already happening and will accelerate in the future.
