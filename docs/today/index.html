Harnesses used to be a crutchn. 拐杖（权宜依靠） for weak LLMs. In 2026 and onward, with stronger models, harnesses now offer a mechanism for composing an LLM's existing and new capabilities to generalize well beyond their training environments.

… A common idea floating around is that LLMs will absorb harnesses and make them obsoleteadj. 过时的. We disagree, and argue that we haven't sufficiently explored what harnesses can provide.

The full paper, logs, codebase, and data are available

💡 论文名 *LM Harnesses are Compositional Generalizers*（作者 Alex Zhang、Souradip Chakraborty、Omar Khattab，就是 DSPy 的作者）。“harness”指包在模型外面的那层框架：工具调用、子 agent、循环等。Sebastian Raschka 补充：模型本来就是在特定 harness 里微调的，所以 harness 不会被模型“吸收”（[链接](https://x.com/rasbt/status/2123853084216455549)）。
