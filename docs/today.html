First hot take: I tell everyone to slow down. … I strongly believe in pacingv. 控制节奏 AI outputs to human speeds. … almost nobody is _actually_ going fast with AI. Engineering still moves at engineering speeds, and business moves even slower.

My agents have, at times, overwhelmed my players with features, my accountant with emails, my merge queue合并队列（等待合入主分支的代码排队） with tickets, and my machines with load. … they've launched broken OSS without permission.

Hot take #2: Should you buy a software factory? … No. … Factories take an extraordinary amount of upkeepn. 维护. They turn into garbage mountains unless you put a frightening amount of time and tokens into curatingv. 精心整理 them. I had to burn my previous five factories down …
