Lauren (poteto): adversarial AI code reviews are mostly a waste of tokens, so she cuts them from her pstack toolkit

@poteto·♥Likes: 9.6k·Original·Nov 3, 02:04 UTC

maybe a hot take but i now consider adversarial reviewsn. 对抗式评审：让一个 agent 专门挑另一个 agent 的毛病 mostly a waste of tokens. far better to use those tokens on empiricaladj. 靠实际运行和实验验证的 combined with formal verificationn. 形式化验证：用数学方法证明代码满足规格. i have updated pstack to use the interrogate skill less, and eventually i think i may remove it altogether

i have found very little value with code review in the abstract, agents tend to just get lost in the nitpicksn. 吹毛求疵的小问题 and yagnisn. YAGNI（You Aren't Gonna Need It）：为用不到的需求过度设计

💡 pstack 是她开源的一套编码 agent 工作流，这两天很多人在试。她的意思是：让 agent 互相"挑刺"很烧 token 又抓不到重点，不如多花在真跑测试和形式化验证上。这是她自己的经验判断，不是实验数据。

levelsio: Claude Code helped cut his business costs by about $34,000 a month, to roughly $300/mo for all his sites

@levelsio·♥Likes: 2.8k·Original·Nov 3, 19:03 UTC

💸 Update: My total biz expenses have now dropped to just ~$300/mo!

This is to run all my websites/apps serving per year: 20,000 paying customers, 252 million visitors, 2.4 billion requests, 2.4 petabyte (PB) in bandwidth

(~$8,000/mo if you add the $7,000/mo GPU bill and $800/mo R2 storage I pay for Photo AI …)

Claude Code helped me to find smart ways to save more money, AI is just really really really good at finding cost savings by itself …

- $29,000/mo - switched to better and cheaper video models on Photo AI … This made my GPU bill go from $36,000/mo to $7,000/mo

- $3,000/mo - consolidated my servers, cancelled Digital Ocean and Linode and moved everything to Hetzner

- $1100/mo - analyzed my Backblaze B2 server backups, I had infinite versioning and lifecycle policies, that's reduced now to 30 days …

- $100/mo - Claude also analyzed my Claude subscription (yes!) and discovered I don't even use the Max 20x plan limits, so I downgraded

💡 最值得学的是思路：把账单、备份策略、域名清单交给 agent 逐项审计。最大的一笔（视频模型从贵的换成便宜的，省 $29k/月）其实是他很久没更新过的配置。数字都是他自己报的。
