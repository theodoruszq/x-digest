"Cognitive surrender": in a study of 1,372 people, wrong AI answers cut accuracy — yet made them more confident

@thesupermannx·♥Likes: 2.4k·Original·Nov 22, 10:30 UTC

Researchers ran three experiments across 1,372 people and 9,593 reasoning trials. They gave participants access to an AI assistant, but secretly riggedv. 暗中操纵 the AI to be accurate on some trials and subtlyadv. 不易察觉地 wrong on others.

People consultedv. 求助于 the AI on more than 50% of the trials. When the AI was right, human accuracy jumped by 25 percentage points. When the AI was wrong, accuracy crashed by 15 percentage points. …

Using the AI made people measurably more confident in their answers—even when the AI fed them completely wrong information.

The researchers tried to break the habit. They added time pressure. They gave participants live feedback and financial incentives … It didn't work.

💡 据 X 新闻汇总，这是 Steven Shaw 和 Gideon Nave 的 2026 年预印本（还没经过同行评审）。发帖人结尾那句“AI 正在悄悄取代思考本身”是他自己的引申。实用启示：先写下自己的答案再问 AI，并对 AI 的回答主动找反例。

MIT theorist Ryan Williams on the P vs NP frenzy: if AI cracks it soon, the answer is probably P=NP

@rrwilliams·♥Likes: 2.6k·Original·Nov 22, 21:45 UTC

(1) If P vs NP is resolved soon (e.g., next few months?) I think that requires P=NP. In that case, my bet is that this will mean "polynomial timen. 多项式时间（理论上“算得快”的标准）" doesn't fit our intuition like we hoped … define a better P vs NP that allows for some kind of ultrafinitistadj. 极端有限主义的（不承认“大到算不完”的数） tuning, which more accurately captures "can every quickly verifiable problem be solved quickly?"

(2) If P!=NP, I highly doubt this will be proved soon, even with AI development accelerating. … We simply don't have hundreds of years of literature to apply … I do think AI will help speed up the development of that literature by orders of magnitude好几个数量级.

(3) Third option: somehow P vs NP can't be resolved using "standard" math, like ZFCn. ZFC 集合论（现代数学的标准公理体系）. This currently seems to be the least likely option.

💡 背景：有个 vibe coder 开直播，让一群 AI agent 在 pequalnp.com 上合作攻 P vs NP，每一步用 Lean 定理证明器自动检查（Polymarket 的报道：[链接](https://x.com/Polymarket/status/2124697909333746059)）；据 X 新闻汇总，目前没有突破。P vs NP 问的是“容易验证的问题是否也容易解”。Williams 是复杂度理论专家，这串帖是业内人对“AI 会不会很快解决千禧难题”最清醒的回应。
