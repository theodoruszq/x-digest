Amdahl's law: why 10x faster coding with AI doesn't make your team ship 10x faster

@arpit_bhayani·♥Likes: 995·Original·Oct 23, 12:39 UTC

The law says the overall speedup is cappedv. 被封顶、限制住 by the part you did not speed up… Say writing code is 30% of the time from idea to production, and AI makes that part 10x faster. The overall speedup is 1 / (0.7 + 0.03), which is about 1.37x. Even if coding is half of the cycle, you get about 1.8x.

… Faster code generation increases the load on every stage after it, so the review queue grows and the bottleneckn. 瓶颈 shifts.

So if you want the team to actually ship faster, measure the whole path from idea to production and find where work waits. Then apply AI there too: review, test generation, flaky testn. 时好时坏、不稳定的测试 triagen. 分类排查（先判断轻重缓急）, deploy safety checks.

💡 Amdahl 定律是 1967 年的老公式：整体加速受限于没被加速的那部分。实用结论是别只用 AI 写代码，要把 AI 用在需求、评审、测试、部署这些"排队等待"的环节。

Jon Barron: every softmax and cross-entropy implementation (PyTorch, JAX, textbooks) has two subtle bugs

@jon_barron·♥Likes: 4.2k·Original·Oct 23, 19:03 UTC

It turns out that *all existing implementations* of softmax and cross-entropy (that's JAX, PyTorch, textbooks, etc) have a subtleadj. 不易察觉的 bug --- two bugs, actually. A simple rewrite fixes both, and eliminates "slingshots" when grokkingn. 顿悟（模型训练很久后突然学会规律的现象）

The first bug is "Softmax Collapse"… When one logitn. 模型输出的原始分数（还没变成概率） is much larger than the others, its gradientn. 梯度（训练时告诉参数往哪调的信号） silently rounds to zero… but the others *don't*, causing gradients to not sum to zero.

💡 Jon Barron 是 Google DeepMind 研究员。softmax 和交叉熵是几乎所有神经网络都要用的两个基础函数，问题出在浮点数精度：小数被悄悄四舍五入成 0，导致训练时损失突然飙升。alphaXiv 的论文摘要（♥391）：在小规模实验里，修复后测试准确率从 94.3% 提到 100%，做蒸馏时学生模型也不会偏离老师。据 X 新闻汇总，Gemini 帮忙发现了问题并起草了论文。
