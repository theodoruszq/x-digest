Lauren Tan: an "inverse pyramid倒金字塔" for trusting your agents' code

@poteto·♥Likes: 4.2k·Original·Oct 16, 16:32 UTC

how do you trust your agents' work more? copy this post/link and give it to your @bot or agent!

1. codebase improvements: … better architecture, stronger types and invariantsn. 不变式（代码里永远必须成立的条件）, clear boundaries. agents love to copy what exists and take the shortest path, so make the easy thing the right thing. categoricallyadv. 彻底地、从根本上 eliminate problems through code and you won't need to think about it again.

2. static analysis: lint rules, tests, CI, verification (eg TLA+). very token efficient ways to catch issues ahead of time.

3. prose: comments, rules, AGENTS.md, skills - anything that can't be enforced and requires agent judgment. use it minimally …

4. human review: the hardest to scale. … spend that effort on the highest leveragen. 杠杆（投入少、收益大） point, designing an environment where agents do the right thing by default.

this forms an inverse pyramid: you want most of your effort at the top, least at the bottom. codebase improvements are deterministicadj. 确定性的（每次结果都一样） …

💡 收藏 7.7k，几乎是点赞的两倍。昨天她分享了 pstack，今天讲的是背后的思路：与其靠写规则、靠人审代码，不如先把代码库本身改得"智能体照着抄也不会错"。帖子后半段还给了一段可以直接交给智能体的提示词：让它翻你过去的代码评审意见和对话记录，找出智能体最常犯的错，再提出改造方案。Morgan Linton 说把这条丢给自己的 Grok Bot，它马上学会了，还举出了一次本可以避免的错误（♥ 706）。
