Satya Nadella: treat AI models like insider risks, and keep the controls outside the model

@satyanadella·♥Likes: 6.4k·Original·Oct 10, 14:43 UTC

…we are deploying these complex agentic systems and models, with access to our most sensitive data and giving them the ability to take mission-criticaladj. 关键任务的，出错代价极高的 actions on our behalf! … we need to separate the supply of intelligence from the authority over it.

Treating frontier closed and open weight models like insider risksn. 内部人员风险（"内鬼"风险） is a way to build such a system. Not because they are necessarily maliciousadj. 恶意的, but because any sufficiently capable actor with access to important systems can make mistakes or be compromisedadj. 被攻破的，被入侵的…

…the controls that govern what a model can access and what actions it can take must sit outside the model.

Containment: We must assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start. Think of it like an emergency brake. An authorized person should always be able to pause or shut down a model mid-task.

The most trustworthy Super Intelligence system will not be the one with the model we trust most. It will be the one that enables us to trust the model the least.

💡 Nadella 是微软 CEO，这是他在 X 上发的长文《Models as Insider Risks in the Super Intelligence Era》，收藏 6.9k，比点赞还多。核心意思：别指望模型自己可靠，要像防"内鬼"一样给 AI 限权限、留日志、随时能叫停。Musk 转发称"Interesting piece"（♥ 14.6k），Chamath 也称这是"非常重要的观察"。

Boris Cherny's recipe for letting Claude optimize things on its own

@bcherny·♥Likes: 35·Original·Oct 10, 21:04 UTC

Claude was able to do this better than I could since Opus 4.8 or so. 5.5 models are now very good at it.

All you do is give the model 1. a goal (eg. "Make p95第 95 百分位，即最慢的 5% 之外 ci time <10 mins") 2. a way to measure its result (eg. "Read timings in bigquery after each change") 3. a sense of how hard to try (eg. "Don't stop till it's done, it might take a few weeks of experimenting")

This pretty much works for most things. I often also ask the model to show me a chart with progress to goal every day, or to dm me a live artifact with the result

💡 Boris Cherny 是 Claude Code 的创建者。这是他回复 Paul Kinlan（"直接说'构建太慢，让它更快'，工具就会去做"）的帖子，点赞不多但收藏 51，干货：目标 + 可量化的检验方式 + 努力程度，三样给齐就能让 AI 自己反复试验。
